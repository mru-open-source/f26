## Sections
 
### Abstract
In one or two concise paragraphs, summarize your relationship to open source and how it changed over the course of the semester.

### Previous experience
Where did you start from? Tell me about your experience prior to working on the projects in this course.

### Your project
You evaluated several projects and ultimately chose one to pursue. You may have switched at some point, if you encountered difficulties working with your original communities, but hopefully you found a community to join and contribute to in a meaningful way.

In this section, describe the project(s) you engaged with in parts 2 and 3. Include what the project is and why you chose to contribute to it.

#### Challenges
In the course of engaging with the community and finding places to contribute, what challenges did you encounter? How did you overcome these challenges?

#### Successes
Share your happy moments! What was successful in your engagement with the community?

### Plans for the future
One of my main objectives in this course is to equip you with the tools and confidence to participate in open source projects in the future. This is tough to do while your time is filled with schoolwork and (and work work), but assuming you had infinite time, energy, and motivation: where do you see your future in open source? How will you use what you've learned from this course in the future?

It's okay if you've learned that open source is not your cup of tea! Just tell me about your future plans regardless of whether your experience was more or less inspiring.

### Final Thoughts
Share any additional comments, learnings, thoughts, etc that you have about the course or open source in general.
