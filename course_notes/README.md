## Course notes
This directory contains student-authored notes taken from each lecture session. To sign up as the notetaker for a session, assign yourself to one of the [issues](https://github.com/mru-open-source/f26/issues) in the list.

### Instructions: Author
When it is your topic, please take notes during class and write a summary of the discussion in [Markdown format](https://markdownformatting.com/). You may do additional research and add some of your thoughts, but this is not required. Total size should be in the 500 word range, and the use of tables and figures is encouraged where appropriate. 

**To submit**: create a fork of this repository and write your course notes in the `course_notes` directory. Name your file `dd-mm-title.md`, where `dd-mm` corresponds to the 2-digit date and month (e.g. `08-09` for September 8th). `title` should be a short descriptive title of your choice, but **do not put spaces in the filename**.Then, edit the index below with a link to your file and submit a pull request with your changes. There should be two changes in the `course_notes` directory: your new `dd-mm-title.md` file, and your addition to this `README.md` file.

Example of index entry:

```markdown
| Date   | Topic                          |
| ------ | ------------------------------ |
| Sept 9 | [Introduction](09-09-intro.md) |
```

**Working with your reviewer**: After submitting, wait for feedback from your reviewer and make any changes requested. You do not need to re-create a pull request, just make your changes and push them and the PR will update.

### Instructions: Reviewer
When you have chosen to (or are assigned to) review a topic, I will give you the power to merge the pull request. Before you do this, you must read through the submission, suggest any changes or corrections, and ensure that the submission meets the requirements. When the author has made changes to your satisfaction, click the "merge" button.

> [!NOTE]
> Reviewing seems like an "easier" task than authoring, but as a reviewer, you are equally responsible for the correctness of the submission.

## Rubric
You will be evaluated on the correctness and completeness of your submission according to the following 4-point scale. Both author and reviewer will receive the same score, unless there is a clear and documented effort imbalance.

| Score | Description                                                            |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 4     | Excellent — thoughtful and creative without any errors or omissions    |
| 3     | Pretty good, but with minor errors or omissions                        |
| 2     | Mostly complete, but with major errors or omissions, lacking in detail |
| 1     | A minimal effort was made, incomplete or incorrect                     |
| 0     | No effort was made, plagiarized, or obviously AI                       |
